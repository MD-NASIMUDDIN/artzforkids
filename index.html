About the studio

25 years of illustration & design, 8 years teaching children in Ireland.

Niamh spent 25 years illustrating for a living before starting ArtzforKids — the after-school classes and camps you'll find running in schools like Scoil Bhríde, Teltown and around Leixlip, Ratoath and Ashbourne. Ellen and Aaron teach some of the sessions too, and parents often mention how quickly the kids warm up to them.

There's usually a running story behind each project — one class spent weeks following "Roller Cat's adventures" — so the drawing and painting has a reason behind it, not just a template to copy.

Frequently asked questions

Everything parents usually ask before booking a class or camp.

What age group are the classes for?
Our classes and camps are designed for children from about 4 to 12 years old, with activities adapted so every age level feels challenged and supported.
Do you offer weekly classes and holiday camps?
Yes — we run weekly after-school art classes during term time in schools across Dublin and Meath, plus Easter and Summer camps for full days of drawing, painting and storytelling.
Does my child need any art experience?
Not at all. Every lesson is follow-along and structured so complete beginners and confident young artists can both join in and enjoy it at their own pace.
What materials are used, and are they included?
All art materials — paints, pencils, paper and finishing touches like frames — are provided as part of the class or camp fee, so you don't need to bring anything.
How do I book a trial class or camp spot?
Message us on Facebook or use the contact details below with your child's age and school/area, and we'll confirm the next available class or camp — spaces fill up quickly each term.
